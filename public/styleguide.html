00 / Direction

Decisions first.

Recorded reasoning from SPEC.md reflected directly in the storefront UI.

Completed Brand Strategy — TENSILE

Audience

The Structural Minimalist (Ages 19–28)

Primary persona Liam (27, Industrial Designer) and secondary persona Maya (22, Studio Printmaker) need a heavyweight white tee ($42 USD) that holds its boxy silhouette under overshirts and backpacks without sheer show-through or collar distortion.

Archetype

The Creator / The Craftsman

Gives TENSILE an unembellished, architectural personality. Rather than lifestyle hype, the brand treats a plain white T-shirt like a precision-engineered studio tool backed by explicit dimensional data.

Visual direction

Modernist Precision (Chosen over Postmodernist)

We compared two visual previews of the same $42 USD hypothetical 280 GSM tee offer: a Swiss Modernist specification grid (selected) vs. a Postmodernist deconstructed collage (rejected because visual noise obscured sizing clarity).
