The Brand Decision

Why TENSILE serves
the Structural Minimalist.

A plain white T-shirt becomes an architectural staple when backed by transparent dimensional metrics and unembellished grid hierarchy.

Primary Persona — Liam (27, Industrial Designer): Needs a crisp, heavyweight white tee ($42 USD) that maintains a structured neckline and boxy drape under overshirts and studio jackets.

Secondary Persona — Maya (22, Studio Printmaker): Looks for tight-knit opaque fabric with explicit pit-to-pit chest measurements (S: 21", M: 22.5", L: 24", XL: 25.5") and honest hypothetical shrinkage disclosures (<1% pre-shrunk concept spec).

Archetype — The Creator / The Craftsman: Precise, architectural, and unembellished. Full documentation is recorded in our TENSILE SPEC.md record .

  6. 06 / Keep

Visual Direction Previews

Two visual previews
of the same offer.

Both visual previews below present the exact same product, hypothetical 280 GSM specification, $42 USD price, and primary action for our Structural Minimalist audience.

Preview 1: Modernist Approach (Chosen for TENSILE)

Offer Preview: TENSILE 001 — Engineered Heavyweight Cotton Tee | $42 USD (Hypothetical 280 GSM combed cotton, S–XL chest specs, "Add to demo bag").

Why it won (Inspired by Uniqlo U & Josef Müller-Brockmann): A disciplined 2-column Swiss grid and high-contrast slate ink (`#0f172a`) let industrial designer Liam scan chest pit-to-pit measurements and collar ribbing specs in seconds on a 375px phone screen.

Preview 2: Postmodernist Approach (Explored & Rejected)

Offer Preview: TENSILE // RAW CUT 001 — Heavyweight Cotton Tee | $42 USD (Hypothetical 280 GSM combed cotton, S–XL chest specs, "Add to demo bag").

Why we rejected it (Inspired by 1990s Ray Gun & brutalist streetwear zines): Overlapping rotated typography, neon hazard borders, and asymmetric layering created expressive energy, but buried the sizing table and reduced mobile scannability for utility-focused buyers.

Comparison outcome: The Modernist grid directly supports honest persuasion by making hypothetical fabric specs and sizing transparent without visual friction.

Connected Reasoning

A reason behind
every visible choice.

How TENSILE connects persona needs to concrete interface decisions.

Audience Need

Liam (27) and Maya (22) shop on mobile and refuse to buy white tees that cling, show through, or hide garment dimensions.

Brand Choice

Pair the $42 USD price and S–XL radio buttons directly with explicit chest measurements (21"–25.5") and labeled hypothetical 280 GSM specs.

Visible Page Verification

Verified at 375px mobile width that the price, size selector, and "Add to demo bag" button remain stacked in one clear viewport without horizontal scrolling.

Verification & Honesty

Truthful claims.
Tested code.

All material and performance statements—including 280 GSM fabric weight, 1.25" bound ribbing collar retention, and <1% pre-shrunk dimensional tolerance—are explicitly labeled as hypothetical classroom specifications rather than verified laboratory proofs.

Automated Playwright tests (`npm test`) and code quality checks (`npm run check`) verify HTML validity, CSS token usage, accessibility labels, and responsive behavior across `index.html`, `styleguide.html`, and `studio.html`.

Core Brand Principle

"State the exact hypothetical specification ($42 USD, 280 GSM concept, pit-to-pit sizing) so the buyer never has to guess."

— TENSILE Design System
