# Brand Specification: TENSILE

- **Audience / Primary Persona:** Liam, 27, Industrial Designer. Needs a crisp, heavyweight white tee that stays structured under structured overshirts.
- **Audience / Secondary Persona:** Maya, 22, Studio Printmaker. Looks for durable, tight-knit fabric with precise sizing and dimensional measurements.
- **Brand Name:** TENSILE
- **Brand Promise:** "Engineered 280 GSM heavyweight cotton. Structure that holds its shape, never clings, and never shows through."
- **Brand Archetype:** The Creator / The Craftsman
- **Voice Traits:** Precise, Architectural, Unembellished

# T-Shirt Research

## 1. Competitor Analysis & Teardowns

### Site 1: Lady White Co. (ladywhiteco.com) — Accessed Nov 2026

- **Observation:** Transparently displays technical garment specifications—specifically 100% domestic cotton origin, cut-and-sew manufacturing in Los Angeles, and exact fabric weight—prominently above the purchase call-to-action without lifestyle gimmicks.
- **What it suggests for TENSILE:** Technical clarity and explicit material specs (like our 280 GSM combed cotton) establish immediate credibility with shoppers who prioritize garment longevity over disposable basics.

### Site 2: Los Angeles Apparel (losangelesapparel.net) — Accessed Nov 2026

- **Observation:** Employs stark, high-contrast, unretouched photography on neutral backgrounds accompanied by straightforward, tabular sizing and measurement specifications.
- **What it suggests for TENSILE:** A utilitarian visual aesthetic reinforces garment structure, opacity, and boxy silhouette without distracting promotional clutter.

### Site 3: Uniqlo U (uniqlo.com) — Accessed Nov 2026

- **Observation:** Uses interactive size-prediction calculators, customer review filtering by height and weight, and visual measurement diagrams.
- **What it suggests for TENSILE:** Digital apparel storefronts must provide unambiguous physical sizing metrics (collar ribbing width, chest pit-to-pit, body length) to prevent customer uncertainty.

## 2. Market Positioning & Audience Hypotheses

### Positioning Comparison

- **Position A (Disposable Commodity):** High-volume multipacks (140–160 GSM) priced under $20. Focuses on low friction, rapid replacement, and lightweight sheer drape.
- **Position B (Architectural Essential — Selected for TENSILE):** Single heavyweight (280 GSM) combed cotton t-shirt priced at $42. Focuses on structural integrity, high opacity, clean boxy drop-shoulder silhouette, and collar shape retention through dozens of wash cycles.

### Persona Hypotheses

- **Persona 1 (The Seasonal Fast-Fashion Buyer):** Buys bulk multipacks for convenience; views t-shirts as hidden undershirts; accepts frequent color fading and collar baconing.
- **Persona 2 (The Structural Minimalist — Selected Audience):** Design-conscious students and young professionals (ages 19–28) who prioritize modular capsule wardrobes and durable streetwear staples. They demand garments that hold their shape under backpacks, resist wrinkling, and drape cleanly as an outer layer.

## 3. Brand Identity & Honest Persuasion

- **Look & Feel:** High-contrast neutral palette (`#0f172a` deep slate, `#f8fafc` crisp off-white, `#64748b` border slate), clean geometric sans-serif typography, and modular grid cards.
- **Brand Character:** Durable, functional, restrained, unembellished.
- **Honest Persuasion Principles Applied:**
  1. _Empirical Specification (Informed Decision-Making):_ Clearly stating the 280 GSM fabric weight, yarn count, and shrinkage tolerances so customers understand exact product density rather than relying on subjective buzzwords like "ultra-soft."
  2. _Transparent Garment Dimensions (Commitment & Confidence):_ Providing explicit pit-to-pit and body length measurements to eliminate guesswork and minimize return waste.
